So, what is RustTD6?

Bloons TD 6, rebuilt from scratch. It uses the art and sounds from your own copy of the game, then runs on its own engine.

  • Increased performance
    5× the average FPS, with more graphics options: an uncapped frame rate, VSync, texture resolution and custom aspect ratios.
  • Private co-op
    Play with friends over an encrypted peer-to-peer room. The host can even share their mods with everyone in it.
  • Everything unlocked
    Every tower, upgrade and hero is available from the start. Monkey XP still counts up, so you can see which towers you use most.

FAQ

Do I need to own BTD6?

Yes. RustTD6 doesn't include the game: it reads the art and sounds from your own copy. RustTD6 itself is free.

Will it run on my PC?

It needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), BTD6 from Steam or Epic, 8 GB of RAM, OpenGL 3.1 graphics, and about 7 GB of disk space (15 GB free while the first setup runs).

Is it finished?

Not yet. Nearly every tower and hero already plays exactly like the real game, but bosses, some game modes, co-op and parts of the visuals are still in progress. See Progress for where things stand.

Why are some modes missing?

The store and online modes like Races, Boss Rush, Contested Territory and Odyssey have been removed, along with the Legends DLC, to prevent cheating and piracy.

Does it change my BTD6 save or account?

No. RustTD6 only reads your BTD6 files and keeps its own progress. It only contacts GitHub, python.org and PyPI, never Ninja Kiwi, and it never changes BTD6, your save or your account.

Can I play it on a Mac or Linux?

Yes, with a personal build. Set it up on Windows first, copy the extracted game data across, and run the build script for your system. The steps are in the README.

Windows or my antivirus warned me. Is it safe?

Warnings are normal for new free programs that aren't code-signed yet, which is a paid certificate. RustTD6 is open source: anyone can read exactly what it does on GitHub, and every release is built automatically from that code. On "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. If your antivirus removes it, restore it and allow the RustTD6 folder.

Where can I get help or report a bug?

Ask on Discord, or open an issue on GitHub. Say what happened and what you expected, with a screenshot if it helps.
