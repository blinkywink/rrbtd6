Install

Three steps. The launcher does the heavy lifting.

  1. 1

    Get MelonLoader

    Install MelonLoader 0.7.3 into BTD6 and run the game once.

  2. 2

    Drop in the installer

    Put the downloaded RRBTD6Installer.dll in BTD6's Mods folder.

  3. 3

    Pick a folder & play

    Open BTD6, choose where RustTD6 goes, and the launcher prepares everything from your game.

First setup time and disk space depend on your machine; clean-install measurements are still pending.

How it works

Not a mod on top of BTD6 — a separate client that borrows your game's art and sound.

It reads your copy of BTD6

The small installer runs inside your game through MelonLoader. On first setup it unpacks the textures, models, sounds and tower data from your install into a local folder. Nothing from the game is ever downloaded from us — that's why you need to own it.

A new engine runs the game

Bloons, towers, projectiles and pops are simulated by an engine written from scratch in Rust. Each tower is checked against recordings of the real game, frame by frame, until both play out the same.

Its own renderer draws it

The client draws everything itself instead of going through the original game, which is where the uncapped frame rate and the extra graphics and menu settings come from.

Questions

Do I need to own BTD6?

Yes. RustTD6 ships no game files. It builds everything it needs from your own installed copy.

Is it finished?

Not yet. It's in active development and some features are unfinished, so expect rough edges.

Is it free?

Completely free and open source.

Is this made by Ninja Kiwi?

No. It's an unofficial fan project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Ninja Kiwi.
